Elle Brauchle

I’m an architect turned product manager. I spent six years at SmithGroup — starting as a designer, finishing as an associate and project manager, and becoming a registered California architect along the way. The work I’m proudest of there was a $3.7B, 909,000 SF hospital, where my job was to sit between engineering and design and keep a hundred competing priorities pointed at the same outcome.

Somewhere in that I stopped thinking of myself as someone who draws buildings and started thinking of myself as someone who builds things for users. I ran a research program in an underserved community — 40+ interviews, synthesized into day-in-the-life stories and prioritized opportunity areas that became the project’s actual requirements. I partnered with a data science team on a tool that pulled multi-form outreach data into one interactive dashboard and cut distillation time by 30% across five projects. I set OKRs with regional leadership to make go/no-go calls against a $2M market target. The artifacts were drawings; the job was product.

Now I’m at MIT Sloan, MBA class of 2028, pointing the same habits at software — Product Management Lab, the AI Builder Proseminar, and the things under Projects, which mostly started as something I wanted to exist and couldn’t find. Outside of that I’m usually bikepacking, skiing in the backcountry, on an alpine trad route, or sketching something.
