# Elle Brauchle

Elle Brauchle is a product manager and licensed architect, currently an MBA
candidate at the MIT Sloan School of Management, class of 2028.

Source: Elle's PM resume (October 2026) and the pages of this site. Last
updated 2026-10-13.

## Short bio

Elle is an architect turned product manager. She spent six years at SmithGroup,
an architecture, engineering and planning firm, moving from designer to licensed
architect and lead planner to associate and project manager. She is now at MIT
Sloan pursuing an MBA, class of 2028, and building software projects alongside
the degree.

In her own words: "I'm an architect turned product manager looking to use my
cross-functional collaboration, design, organization, and leadership skills to
build better products for users that need them."

## Career at SmithGroup

SmithGroup is an award-winning architecture, engineering and planning firm with
roughly 1,600 employees and over $400 million in annual revenue. Elle worked
there from 2020 to 2026, in three roles: Designer from 2020 to 2023, Licensed
Architect and Lead Planner from 2023 to 2026, and Associate and Project Manager
from 2025 to 2026.

## Product development and cross-functional work

Elle directed cross-functional coordination between engineering and design on a
$3.7 billion, 909,000 square foot, 14-story hospital, resolving competing
priorities and high-stakes tradeoffs to keep the product at the forefront and
schedules on track.

She facilitated more than 12 sprints and sprint planning sessions with
engineering, marketing and design.

She partnered with data science teams to build a tool that collected and
consolidated multi-form data from user outreach into an interactive dashboard for
owner interaction, cutting distillation time by 30% as measured across five
projects.

She analyzed eight data streams in ArcGIS to model optimal siting and
programmatic uses for the expansion of a 188-acre campus expecting to double
student enrollment over the next decade.

## User research and market research

Elle designed and led a user research program within a targeted underserved
audience. She conducted more than 40 in-person and virtual interviews and
synthesized the findings into day-in-the-life stories, key themes, and
prioritized opportunity areas that became design requirements, shaping the
project and winning client approval.

She evaluated three visual platforms for a client, weighing flexibility, cost and
future management tradeoffs, and prioritized the option that retained the most
control over updates.

She partnered with regional leadership and marketing on quarterly market roadmap
prioritization, assessing competitive positioning and setting OKRs to guide
go/no-go decisions in support of a $2 million annual market target.

## Stakeholder engagement and communication

Elle synthesized complex, multi-source data from user surveys, stakeholder input
and research into visual and written narratives for executive audiences, securing
university leadership's approval for adoption into a campus master plan.

She directed an eight-person team's proposal for a multimillion-dollar urban
planning project, beating six competing firms and securing the firm's first
foothold in a new region and submarket.

She led over a dozen presentations to stakeholders, from hospital executives to
city officials, covering design, functionality and tradeoffs, building alignment
and guiding timely decisions, and resulting in fewer meetings than comparable
projects.

## Management and mentorship

Elle mentored a team of five junior staff across three concurrent projects
through weekly one-on-ones, coaching on communication and client-facing
presentation skills, and contributing to multiple promotions and professional
licensures.

She managed budget and schedule performance across internal staff and four
consultant teams on a $300,000 development, navigating two contract modifications
through fee and scope reevaluation and securing above-average profitability.
