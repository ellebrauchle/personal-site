Petal Ping

  • Solo build
  • 2026

A health tool that pulls sleep metrics, menstrual cycles, and recorded symptoms from the health apps you use, and turns that data into notifications to keep you at your best: a heads-up before a cycle starts, the symptoms that may come with it, research-backed tips, and an alert when a trend appears. It learns as it goes — not just your health data, but how you like to be notified and which alerts you find useful. Your tool, for your life, built to benefit you.

Why I built it

The tools that exist are either too limited or too costly. Apple Health predicts a cycle but barely lets you log a symptom; the apps that do it properly, like Flo, generally want paying. I wanted a free one that fit the way I actually live, and I wanted to learn to build software rather than wait for someone else to build it.

How it fits together

It connects to the health apps a person already uses — Apple Health, Flo, Natural Cycles — rather than asking them to start logging somewhere new. A companion app shows the history, with an export for medical appointments, so a pattern you have noticed is something you can hand to a doctor.

Where it stands

The current sprint, for the AI Builder Space Proseminar at MIT Sloan, is the smallest useful version: pull real cycle and symptom data from a health app, send predictive heads-up notifications, deliver research-backed tips. Trend alerts, the medical export and the full companion app are out of scope for now. Cycle tracking works month by month, so predictive accuracy is not something a single sprint can validate.

Open questions

Which app serves as the data source, given how hard Apple Health is to integrate with. Whether data can be pulled automatically or only by manual export. How often a notification should arrive before it becomes noise. How to fold what a user tells me back into the alerts they get.

It starts as a tool for me, with room to grow for anyone managing periods — especially irregular or difficult ones — and eventually for other things worth tracking.
