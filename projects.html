Petal Ping

A health tool that pulls sleep metrics, menstrual cycles, and symptoms from the health apps you already use, and turns that data into notifications worth reading: a heads-up before a cycle starts, the symptoms that may come with it, research-backed tips, and an alert when a trend appears. It learns as it goes — not just your health data, but how you like to be notified and which alerts you actually find useful. Your tool, for your life, built to benefit you.
